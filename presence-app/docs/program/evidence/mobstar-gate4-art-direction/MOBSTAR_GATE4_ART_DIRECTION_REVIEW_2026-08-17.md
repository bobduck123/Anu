# Mobstar Gate 4 art-direction review

## Decision

`RETURN TO HARDENING` after independent scoring. The candidate is a real improvement over Gate 3 and proves the object model can carry richer architecture, lighting, media and rack interactions. It does not clear the 3.5/4 visual acceptance bar.

## Placeholder audit

Gate 3 used nine `procedural-placeholder` components across all 14 placements and six generated placeholder media records. The rack was freestanding, garments/products/posters were planes, lighting was globally fixed, inspection translated a plane, and mobile had semantic cards without a branded spatial composition.

## Candidate result

The separate Gate 4 fixture uses 10 versioned component references and 17 placements: open boutique shell, polished floor, ribbed identity wall, framed logo, sculptural display bay, two rounded islands, long suspended rack, six garment-on-hanger placements, projection grid and archive frame. Lighting and interaction are selected from data; the renderer has no Mobstar branch.

Fresh visual review found:

| Criterion | Independent score /4 | Evidence |
|---|---:|---|
| Silhouette/composition | 2.0 | Recognisable room, but sparse and boxy compared with the reference's curved, product-dense sweep. |
| Scale | 1.0 | Garments/rack read toy-sized and the blank plinth dominates the human-scale composition. |
| Material response | 1.0 | Dark/cream separation exists, but polished floor, plaster, ribbing and metal lack convincing physical depth. |
| Lighting hierarchy | 2.0 | Warm pools exist, but blacks are crushed and blank geometry often dominates product. |
| Mobstar brand fit | 2.0 | Logo, palette and campaign imagery are present but feel applied rather than architecturally integrated. |
| Mobile readability | 3.0 | Branded static content and semantics are legible in the compact viewport, though operator chrome precedes the visitor view. |
| Interaction | 2.0 | Controls work and return is deterministic, but the outward garment turn is barely perceptible. |

Independent average: **1.86/4**. Atmosphere also scored **2/4**. This is below 3.5, six categories are below 3, and neither atmosphere nor interaction reaches 4. Gate 4 is therefore not accepted.
