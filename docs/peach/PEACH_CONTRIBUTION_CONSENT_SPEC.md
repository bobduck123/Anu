# PEACH Contribution And Consent Specification

## Purpose

PEACH invites Contributions so a Field can be tended through reading, conversation, research, memory, and making. Contributions are not social posts. They are consented submissions into a stewarded cultural process.

No Contribution may appear publicly or be used in a Yield without steward review and matching consent.

## Contribution Types

A Contribution may be:

- text reflection;
- question;
- memory;
- image;
- audio;
- video link;
- file upload;
- research note;
- artwork;
- interview note;
- archive fragment;
- other steward-defined material.

Books may be referenced as Vessels or research notes, but the contribution system must not hard-code books as the center of PEACH.

## Required Contribution Fields

- Field association.
- Tending prompt association, if submitted through a prompt.
- Contributor name or chosen credit.
- Contact method if required for follow-up, rights, or withdrawal.
- Contribution type.
- Body, file, or link.
- Visibility preference.
- Consent level.
- Credit preference.
- Permission for use in Yield.
- Sensitive material flag.
- Review status.
- Withdrawal/contact process acknowledgement.
- Submitted date.
- Consent record version.

## Consent Levels

| Level | Label | Meaning | Public use allowed? | Yield use allowed? | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Private to stewards only | Steward may read for context and care. | No. | No. | Default for sensitive material unless contributor chooses otherwise. |
| 2 | May be discussed internally | Steward/maker team may discuss internally for Field interpretation. | No. | No direct quotation or inclusion. | Can inform themes without identifiable use. |
| 3 | May be quoted anonymously | Material may be quoted without public identifying credit. | Yes, anonymous only. | Only if Yield permission is also granted or included in this consent choice. | Remove identifying details where needed. |
| 4 | May be publicly credited | Material may appear with chosen public credit. | Yes. | Not automatically; Yield permission still required. | Credit must match contributor preference. |
| 5 | May be included in a Yield | Material may be edited/included in the Field Yield within stated scope. | Yes, according to credit preference. | Yes. | Requires final check before release. |
| 6 | Requires follow-up before publication | Steward must contact contributor before any public or Yield use. | Not until follow-up completed. | Not until follow-up completed. | Use for unclear, sensitive, youth-related, rights-complex, or high-stakes material. |

## Visibility Preferences

- Private: stewards only.
- Internal: steward/maker team only.
- Anonymous public: may appear without identifying credit.
- Credited public: may appear with chosen credit.
- Yield-only: may appear only in the produced Yield, not as standalone public page material.
- Follow-up required: no public use until steward confirms.

## Credit Preferences

- Full name.
- Chosen name.
- Organization/group name.
- Pseudonym.
- Anonymous.
- Credit withheld for safety.
- Follow up before crediting.

## Submission Flow

1. Participant enters through a Field page and chooses a Tending prompt or general Field contribution path.
2. Participant reads a plain-language notice: the Contribution will be reviewed, will not be public immediately, and will only be used according to consent.
3. Participant selects contribution type and provides body, file, or link.
4. Participant chooses visibility preference, consent level, credit preference, and Yield permission.
5. Participant marks whether the material is sensitive, involves another person, includes minors/youth, or has rights/permission constraints.
6. Participant confirms contact/withdrawal process.
7. System records submission as Pending and sends confirmation if contact method is available.
8. Steward reviews before any public display or use.

## Review Flow

1. Steward opens Pending queue.
2. Steward checks Field/prompt fit, safety, rights, sensitivity, consent level, and credit preference.
3. Steward marks review state: Accepted, Held, Rejected, Private, Selected, Withdrawn, or Published.
4. If the material is Selected for Yield, steward performs a final consent/credit check.
5. If consent is Level 6 or unclear, steward requests follow-up and blocks publication until resolved.
6. Steward records decision reason and any contributor notification.

## Approval States

- Pending: submitted, not reviewed.
- Held: needs more context, follow-up, rights check, or safety review.
- Accepted: may inform Field work within consent.
- Selected: intended for Yield, pending final checks.
- Rejected: will not be used.
- Private: steward-only record.
- Withdrawn: contributor has requested removal or use has been stopped.
- Published: visible publicly within consent.

## Public And Private Handling

- Public Field pages may show only Published material.
- Yield drafts may include only material that is Accepted or Selected and permitted for maker access.
- Private material may inform steward understanding only if consent allows.
- Sensitive material should default to private or follow-up-required handling.
- Rejected material should not be displayed, quoted, or summarized in identifiable form.
- Withdrawn material must be removed from future use where feasible and flagged in audit records.

## Youth/Child Warning Placeholder

Phase 1 must include a visible warning placeholder: if a Contribution is from, about, or includes a child or young person, the contributor must disclose this and the steward must review before any publication or Yield use.

Until a dedicated youth safeguarding policy exists, youth/child material should default to Level 6: Requires follow-up before publication. PEACH must not publish identifiable youth material without explicit guardian/participant consent and a safety review appropriate to the jurisdiction and context.

## Takedown And Withdrawal Process

Contributor rights language must explain:

- how to contact PEACH about a Contribution;
- what can be withdrawn before publication;
- what can be corrected or removed after publication;
- that some already-distributed artifacts may not be fully recallable;
- that PEACH will stop future use of withdrawn material where feasible;
- expected response window for takedown requests.

Minimum Phase 1 process:

1. Contributor contacts steward through Field contact path.
2. Steward verifies contributor identity or authority where appropriate.
3. Steward marks Contribution as Withdrawal Requested.
4. Steward removes or restricts material if consent/safety requires.
5. Steward updates Yield/Commons artifacts where feasible.
6. Steward records outcome and communicates back to contributor.

## Steward Responsibilities

Stewards must:

- make consent plain before submission;
- review every Contribution before publication;
- respect credit preferences;
- avoid coercive prompts;
- avoid extracting trauma, memory, images, or oral histories for impact;
- check rights for third-party materials;
- keep private material private;
- document decisions;
- create a path for withdrawal, correction, and questions;
- ensure Yield and Commons use matches consent.

## Contributor Rights Language

Plain-language baseline:

"You choose how your contribution may be used. PEACH will review it before anything is public. You can keep it private to stewards, allow internal discussion, allow anonymous quotation, allow public credit, allow use in the Field Yield, or ask us to follow up before publication. You can contact the steward to ask questions, change permission where possible, request correction, or request withdrawal."

## Anti-Extraction Safeguards

- No public display before review.
- No Yield use without matching consent.
- No sponsor access to private Contributions.
- No sale of personal Contributions as inventory.
- No prompts that pressure disclosure of harm.
- No use of community stories without Return.
- No hidden rights grab in submission copy.
- No permanent waiver that prevents future withdrawal requests.
