# PEACH Gate 14 Real Pilot Pack

Date: 2026-07-31

## Status

Prepared for a real 3-5 trusted-adult pilot. The pilot has not happened.

## Participant invitation

You are invited to a private internal PEACH pilot for trusted adults.

PEACH cultivates society's interest in itself. It opens Fields around Seeds of intrigue - questions, memories, books, places, wounds, contradictions or futures - and gathers people to tend them through reading, conversation, research and making. Each Field may later produce cultural works that return to the Commons.

This is a private rehearsal, not a public launch.

## Consent and safety boundary copy

Before participating, confirm:

- you are an adult;
- you will submit non-sensitive text only;
- you will not submit youth or child material;
- you will not submit sensitive testimony;
- you will not upload files;
- no payment will be taken;
- nothing you submit will be publicly displayed;
- no Commons/Yield release will happen from this pilot;
- a steward may review your submission manually;
- export and withdrawal requests are manual steward review requests, not automatic deletion/export.

## Facilitator script

1. Welcome the participant and restate the private pilot boundary.
2. Read the PEACH definition.
3. Explain that PEACH is not a book club, bookstore, ecommerce storefront, social feed, generic content platform, events site, or subscription clone.
4. Open Field 001: `Studying Ourselves`.
5. Explain Field, Seed, Yield, and Commons Return in plain language.
6. Ask the participant to complete the task list.
7. Ask post-pilot feedback questions.
8. Record only participant label and necessary evidence.

## Field 001 intro

Field 001 is `Studying Ourselves`.

Seed:

`What does a community learn when it studies itself as seriously as institutions study it?`

Plain-language terms:

- Field: a bounded cultural inquiry opened by a Seed.
- Seed: the question or tension that invites attention.
- Yield: a later work made only from reviewed material with the right consent.
- Commons Return: the approved work, method, or learning that comes back to the community after consent and steward review.

## Participant task list

Each participant should:

1. Read the PEACH landing page.
2. Enter Field 001.
3. Explain what they think PEACH is.
4. Explain the Seed in their own words.
5. Submit one safe adult/non-sensitive Contribution.
6. Choose consent, credit, visibility, and Yield permission values.
7. Confirm they understand nothing is public.

At least two participants should:

8. Submit a SupportIntent for `membership`, `sponsor_access`, or `sponsor_yield`.
9. Confirm they understand no payment was taken.

At least one participant should:

10. Submit an export request.

At least one participant or steward should:

11. Test a withdrawal request.

## Contribution prompt

Share one short, non-sensitive reflection, question, memory, or research note in response to:

`What does a community learn when it studies itself as seriously as institutions study it?`

Do not include private third-party information, youth material, sensitive testimony, medical/legal/crisis material, or anything requiring safeguarding.

## Support intent task

Submit a manual SupportIntent. The facilitator must say:

This records interest for follow-up only. No payment is taken. PEACH does not show a checkout, cart, product grid, or payment success screen.

## Consent operation task

Submit an export or withdrawal request. The facilitator must say:

This creates a manual steward review request. It does not automatically export, delete, publish, or withdraw anything.

## Evidence checklist

- participant label only;
- contribution ID;
- ConsentRecord existence;
- support intent ID where applicable;
- consent operation request ID where applicable;
- steward review status;
- audit counts;

## Stop conditions

Stop if:

- youth or child material appears;
- sensitive testimony appears;
- a participant believes payment was taken;
- a participant believes their material is public;
- participant distress appears;
- third-party private information appears;
- facilitator or steward is unsure whether material is safe.
