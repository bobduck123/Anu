# PEACH Field Support Model

## Purpose

PEACH must create income and profit through values-aligned cultural production. Phase 1 support should fund access, stewardship, making, preservation, Commons Return, and future Fields. It must not rely on affiliate/dropship-first commerce, generic storefront flows, or membership as discount perks.

This is a support model, not payment code.

## Required Support Principles

- Money may support access, stewardship, production, preservation, and future Fields.
- Money must not buy editorial control, contributor access, private Contribution access, or predetermined conclusions.
- Supporters and patrons may be acknowledged only according to their preference and PEACH transparency rules.
- Support copy must never make membership feel like a discount club.
- Field support must not become a storefront or product grid.

## Phase 1 Minimum Implementation

Phase 1 can use external payment links, invoices, or manual intake if each transaction can be associated with:

- support path;
- Field ID, if Field-specific;
- supporter/patron contact;
- amount;
- acknowledgement preference;
- intended support category;
- receipt/reference ID;
- date;
- notes about restrictions or editorial-independence acceptance.

## Reporting Minimum

Stewards/admins need to see:

- total support by Field;
- support by path;
- access sponsorship funded and used;
- sponsor/patron acknowledgements;
- restricted vs unrestricted funds;
- production support for Yield;
- unresolved support inquiries.
