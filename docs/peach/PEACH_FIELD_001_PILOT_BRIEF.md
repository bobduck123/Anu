# PEACH Field 001 Pilot Brief

Status: Gate 5 pilot brief

## Field Title

Studying Ourselves

## Seed

What does a community learn when it studies itself as seriously as institutions study it?

## Soil

- Communities are often researched, represented, marketed to, categorized, and reported on by institutions.
- External descriptions can be useful, but they often sit outside the community's own interpretive life.
- This Field asks what changes when memory, reading, archival fragments, conversation, and making become shared practice.
- The Field does not claim to represent a whole community. It rehearses a method for communal self-study.

## Vessels

- Community self-study question set.
- Local or organizational archive fragment.
- Recorded conversation with a community memory-holder.
- Public institutional representation of the community.
- Cultural work made from self-observation.

Books may appear as one Vessel type, but this Field is not a book club. Vessels are prompts for attention, research, conversation, and making.

## Gatherings

- Reading The Outside View: compare how institutions describe a community with what community members notice, value, dispute, or remember.
- Making From The Inside View: select recurring questions, images, phrases, contradictions, and memories from reviewed Contributions and shape the first Yield.

For Gate 5, gatherings can be simulated as one facilitator-led conversation or a written response round.

## Tending Prompts

- What is one question outsiders often ask about this community, and what question do you wish the community asked itself?
- Share one object, place, phrase, document, image, or memory that helps this community recognize itself.
- What has this community learned the hard way that should be preserved for future members?

Pilot guardrail: contributors should avoid trauma disclosure, private third-party details, youth/child material, and material that needs professional support or legal handling.

## Contribution Examples

Suitable:

- "Outsiders ask how big we are. I wish we asked what forms of care keep us coherent."
- "A recurring phrase from our meetings helps us recognize ourselves: we say 'hold the thread' when something matters but is unresolved."
- "A public annual report describes us through activity numbers. It misses the informal mentoring that actually carries the work."

Not suitable for Gate 5:

- detailed trauma testimony,
- allegations against named people,
- child or youth memories,
- medical or legal disclosures,
- private archives without permission,
- files or images requiring rights review.

## Intended Yield

Working title: Studying Ourselves: A First PEACH Field Report.

Pilot shape: an 8 to 12 page digital publication or equivalent web publication with selected consented excerpts, source trail, method notes, and continuation questions.

Gate 5 does not publish the Yield. It only rehearses what steward review would need before a later Commons Return.

## Support-Intent Framing

Support is manual and metadata-only. The pilot support paths are:

- membership,
- one-off support,
- sponsor access,
- sponsor Field,
- sponsor Yield.

No payment is taken. Support intent should be read as a rehearsal of what money could enable: access, stewardship, facilitation, contributor care, archive work, production, and Commons Return.

## Commons Return Simulation

During Gate 5, the steward may prepare a private simulation note:

- what themes emerged,
- what consent would allow,
- what must stay private,
- what would need follow-up,
- what could become a Field Report,
- what questions should return to the Commons.

Nothing is published from pilot contributions.

## Pilot Success Criteria

- Participants understand the Seed as community self-study, not reading group or content submission.
- Contributions create pending review records.
- ConsentRecords exist and carry consent version.
- Steward can inspect consent level, visibility preference, credit preference, Yield permission, sensitive flag, and timestamps.
- No contribution body appears on public pages.
- Support intent remains manual and non-payment.
- The steward can name what Gate 6 must harden before wider use.
