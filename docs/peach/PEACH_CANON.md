# PEACH Canon

## Canonical Statement

"PEACH is a cultural cultivation system. Each Field begins with a Seed of intrigue — a question, memory, book, place, wound, contradiction or future — and gathers people to tend it through reading, conversation, research and making. Each Field produces cultural works that return to the Commons."

## Sharper Thesis

"PEACH cultivates society's interest in itself."

In practical product terms, this means PEACH is not primarily a publishing feed, events calendar, bookstore, or discussion forum. It is a repeatable system for turning communal curiosity into cultural production. The product should help a steward open a Field, frame a Seed, gather Vessels, invite careful Tending, select and edit Contributions, produce a Yield, and Return that Yield to a shared Commons with consent, attribution, and future usefulness.

## What PEACH Is

PEACH is ANU's collective-cultural vertical. It creates spaces where communities study themselves with seriousness, tenderness, and productive intent.

PEACH organizes attention around Fields. A Field is a cultivated area of communal attention opened around a Seed of intrigue. People enter the Field through Vessels such as books, songs, documents, oral histories, images, places, recipes, artworks, archives, or memories. They tend the Field through structured reading, conversation, research, contribution, and making. The Field produces Yields such as essays, annotated archives, exhibitions, publications, recordings, teaching kits, gatherings, zines, maps, performances, or other cultural works. Those Yields Return to the Commons so they can be inherited, taught, revised, and continued.

PEACH is buildable as a product because its core object is specific: the Field. Everything else should support the Field lifecycle.

## What PEACH Is Not

PEACH is not:

- a book club website;
- an online bookstore;
- a Shopify-style store;
- an affiliate or dropship retail project;
- a content platform;
- a generic events or community site;
- a Substack or Patreon clone;
- a social feed;
- a publishing marketplace;
- a CMS with cultural language pasted on top;
- a discount-membership program;
- a place to extract community stories without consent.

Books, events, membership, publishing, archives, and commerce may appear inside PEACH, but none of them is the primitive. The primitive is the Field.

## Why PEACH Belongs On ANU

ANU is building systems for human and civilisational formation. Presence addresses the individual's becoming. PEACH addresses the community's capacity to cultivate, interpret, preserve, and produce culture.

PEACH belongs on ANU because it extends ANU's concern from the individual room to the collective commons:

- Presence helps a person ask, "Who am I becoming?"
- PEACH helps a community ask, "What are we learning about ourselves, and what are we making from that attention?"

Both verticals resist flattening. Presence resists the flattening of personal identity into profiles, feeds, and templates. PEACH resists the flattening of culture into consumable content, retail objects, trends, and engagement loops.

## How PEACH Differs From Presence

Presence is ANU's individual vertical.

- Question: "Who am I becoming?"
- Primitive: Presence / Room / Work.
- Fracture: individual identity has been flattened into profiles, feeds, and templates.
- Product orientation: private or semi-private spaces for attention, identity, practice, and personal work.

PEACH is ANU's collective-cultural vertical.

- Question: "How does a community cultivate interest in itself and turn that curiosity into culture?"
- Primitive: Field.
- Fracture: communities have been trained to consume culture rather than cultivate, interpret, preserve, and produce it.
- Product orientation: stewarded communal spaces that produce cultural Yields for a Commons.

Presence is not the private version of PEACH. PEACH is not the group version of Presence. They are sibling verticals with different primitives, different audiences, and different production loops.

## Civilisational Problem

Communities increasingly encounter themselves through institutions, platforms, markets, and media systems that turn cultural life into data, content, demographics, products, sentiment, nostalgia, or spectacle.

This creates several failures:

- communities consume representations of themselves without learning how to interpret or produce them;
- archives become inaccessible, inert, or extractive;
- memory is either privatized, commercialized, or lost;
- public conversation rewards performance over interpretation;
- cultural participation becomes attendance, posting, purchasing, or fandom rather than contribution and making;
- institutions study communities more seriously than communities are resourced to study themselves.

PEACH addresses this by giving communities a repeatable structure for cultivating interest in themselves and converting that interest into durable cultural works.

## Core Thesis

Culture is not only consumed, discovered, or distributed. It is cultivated.

A community becomes more capable when it can:

- notice its own questions;
- gather source material around those questions;
- interpret that material together;
- invite contributions with consent and care;
- make something from the gathered attention;
- Return that work to a Commons that future people can inherit.

PEACH exists to operationalize that thesis.

## Core Loop

The PEACH core loop is:

1. Open a Field around a Seed of intrigue.
2. Prepare the Soil by naming context, histories, people, places, tensions, and conditions.
3. Gather Vessels that help people enter the Field.
4. Host Gatherings and structured Tending prompts.
5. Review Contributions with consent, safety, editorial care, and attribution.
6. Make a Yield from selected material.
7. Return the Yield to the Commons.
8. Let the Return seed future Fields, teaching, revision, publication, or continuation.

The loop must be visible in the product. If a feature does not help a Field move through this loop, it is not Phase 1 core.

## Working Terms

- Orchard: the whole PEACH environment, including all Fields, Commons, stewards, members, patrons, Yields, and operating practices.
- Field: the core launch object; a cultivated area of communal attention.
- Seed: the central intrigue that opens a Field.
- Soil: the context surrounding the Seed.
- Vessel: source material that helps people enter and tend a Field.
- Tending: structured acts of reading, conversation, research, contribution, and making.
- Gathering: a live, recorded, digital, or asynchronous moment of collective interpretation.
- Contribution: a consented submission into a Field.
- Maker: a person shaping selected material into a Yield.
- Steward: the person or team responsible for care, framing, moderation, and Return.
- Patron: a person or institution supporting access, production, preservation, or a Field.
- Yield: the cultural work produced by a Field.
- Commons: the shared body of returned Yields and usable cultural inheritance.
- Return: the act of placing a Yield back into the Commons with consent and continuity.
- Season: a bounded cycle of Field activity.

These terms are operational language, not decorative language. They should clarify product behavior.

## Income Logic

PEACH must create income and profit, but revenue must come from values-aligned cultural production rather than extractive commerce.

Aligned revenue paths include:

- membership that supports access, participation, and the Commons;
- paid Field sponsorship;
- access sponsorship for participants who could not otherwise join;
- facilitated Field programs for communities, institutions, schools, libraries, cultural organizations, or civic groups;
- commissioned Fields;
- workshops and steward training;
- cultural works, publications, editions, prints, recordings, teaching kits, and exhibitions produced from Fields;
- archive, publication, and editorial services;
- institutional packs that help organizations host Fields responsibly;
- patronage that funds production, preservation, or public access.

Misaligned revenue paths include:

- affiliate-first book commerce;
- dropship merchandise;
- membership as a discount club;
- selling community stories without meaningful consent and return;
- paywalling the Commons so completely that Return becomes private inventory;
- optimizing Fields around retail conversion instead of cultural production.

Revenue is legitimate when it funds cultivation, access, stewardship, making, preservation, and Return.

## Values Boundaries

PEACH must observe these boundaries:

- Consent before use: personal stories, images, oral histories, and contributions require clear consent for collection, editing, publication, and reuse.
- Attribution by default: contributors, makers, stewards, and source holders should be credited unless safety or consent requires anonymity.
- Non-extraction: communities must receive cultural, practical, or economic return when their stories and materials generate value.
- Editorial care: public Contributions must be reviewed before publication.
- Safety: prompts should invite meaningful response without coercing trauma disclosure.
- Specificity: Fields must have a real Seed, not vague community language.
- Production pathway: participation should lead toward interpretation, making, and Return.
- Commons orientation: PEACH should preserve and share Yields in ways that future people can use.
- Revenue transparency: members and patrons should understand what their money supports.
- Metaphor discipline: Orchard, Field, Seed, Soil, and related terms are working concepts. They must not become twee decoration.

## Phase 1 Launch Principle

Phase 1 must prove one excellent Field, not the whole utopian system.

The first launch should demonstrate that PEACH can:

- frame a Seed with tension and reason-for-now;
- gather strong Vessels;
- host at least two meaningful Gatherings;
- invite structured, consented Tending;
- review and select Contributions;
- produce one credible Yield;
- Return that Yield to the Commons;
- show a values-aligned support path.

If Phase 1 can be honestly described as "a book club website with a shop and events," Gate 0 has failed. If it can be described as "a stewarded cultural cultivation system that begins with one excellent Field," Gate 0 has succeeded.
