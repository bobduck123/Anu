# PEACH Phase 1 Scope

## Launch Principle

Phase 1 must prove one excellent Field, not the whole PEACH system.

The launch should demonstrate the complete cultural cultivation loop at a small, credible scale: Seed, Soil, Vessels, Gatherings, Tending prompts, reviewed Contributions, Making, Yield, and Return.

## Phase 1 Must Include

- One active Field with a specific Seed that has tension, specificity, and reason-for-now.
- A public Field page that explains the Seed, Soil, Vessels, Gatherings, Tending prompts, intended Yield, steward, and support path.
- 3-5 Vessels with access notes and source/rights awareness.
- At least two Gatherings tied to interpretation and making.
- 2-3 Tending prompts that invite safe, meaningful, structured Contributions.
- A consented Contribution intake path.
- Steward review before any Contribution is public or used in a Yield.
- A clearly scoped Phase 1 Yield.
- A Return plan that explains how the Yield enters the Commons.
- A values-aligned revenue/support hook.
- Admin-editable Field content and status.

## Phase 1 Must Not Include

- Multiple Fields unless the first Field is complete and excellent.
- Marketplace, bookstore, affiliate, or dropship flows.
- Social feed, follower graph, public comments, likes, or unreviewed posting.
- Full public user profiles.
- Automated moderation as the only safety layer.
- Payment logic beyond simple support links or manual sponsorship intake.
- Database migrations unless a later product/data gate explicitly requires them.
- Full Commons infrastructure beyond what is needed to Return the first Yield.
- Generic brand strategy or marketing pages that do not serve the Field.
- A broad events platform.

## Objects That Must Exist Conceptually

- Orchard
- Field
- Seed
- Soil
- Vessel
- Gathering
- Tending prompt
- Contribution
- Consent record
- Maker
- Steward
- Patron
- Yield
- Commons
- Return
- Season

These objects do not all need full technical implementation in Phase 1, but product and data decisions must not contradict them.

## Objects That Must Be Functional At Launch

- Field: public page and steward/admin editing.
- Seed: displayed and editable.
- Soil: displayed and editable.
- Vessels: displayed and editable.
- Gatherings: displayed and editable with date/time or asynchronous access details.
- Tending prompts: displayed and editable.
- Contribution intake: functional through a form or manual equivalent.
- Consent capture: functional and tied to each Contribution.
- Steward review: functional, even if manual.
- Yield: at least one produced work or work-in-progress page.
- Return plan: displayed publicly before completion and updated when completed.
- Support path: functional through manual sponsorship inquiry, membership link, or existing payment/support infrastructure.

## What Can Remain Manual Or Semi-Manual

- Contribution triage and editorial selection.
- Consent follow-up and withdrawal handling.
- Patron/sponsor onboarding.
- Publication layout and production of the first Yield.
- Commons indexing.
- Gathering reminders and post-Gathering notes.
- Attribution review.
- Rights checks for Vessels.
- Maker coordination.

Manual work is acceptable if the public promise is honest and the stewardship process is reliable.

## What Must Be Admin-Editable

- Field title and status.
- Seed.
- Soil summary and full context.
- Vessels and their metadata.
- Gathering details.
- Tending prompts.
- Steward and Maker information.
- Contribution visibility/use status.
- Yield description and links.
- Return plan and Return status.
- Support/revenue copy and links.
- Access notes and safety/consent copy.

## Launch Success Criteria

Phase 1 succeeds if:

- a new visitor can understand that PEACH is a cultural cultivation system, not a book club or store;
- the Field has a strong Seed and visible production pathway;
- participants can enter through Vessels and Gatherings;
- participants can contribute safely with consent;
- stewards can review and select Contributions;
- at least one credible Yield is produced;
- the Yield Returns to the Commons in a usable form;
- revenue/support is aligned with access, stewardship, production, preservation, or Return;
- the system creates enough evidence to specify deeper product, data, and UI requirements.

## Launch Failure Criteria

Phase 1 fails if:

- the experience can honestly be summarized as "book club plus events";
- the homepage or Field page is dominated by shopping, posting, or generic content;
- the Seed is vague or interchangeable;
- Vessels are just a reading list with no production pathway;
- Gatherings are disconnected from Making and Return;
- Contributions are public before review;
- consent is unclear or absent;
- no credible Yield is produced;
- Return is only a slogan and no usable Commons artifact exists;
- revenue relies on affiliate/dropship mechanics or discount membership;
- steward responsibilities are invisible.

## Gate 1 Readiness

Phase 1 is ready for deeper product, data, and UI specification when the Field V1 format is accepted, the first Field Seed is approved, and the manual boundaries for contribution, consent, making, and Return are explicit.
