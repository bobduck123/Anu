# PEACH Field Public Page Specification

## Purpose

The public Field page is the core Phase 1 experience. It must feel like entering a cultivated area of communal attention: a specific Seed, prepared Soil, selected Vessels, stewarded Gatherings, safe Tending prompts, visible Making, and a Return plan.

It must not feel like an event page, book detail page, blog post, product grid, social feed, or generic community landing page.

## Page-Level Requirements

- The Field, not PEACH brand copy, is the first meaningful object.
- The Seed must be visible before any support, registration, or event action.
- The page must show the production pathway: how attention becomes Yield and Return.
- Contribution entry must explain review and consent before submission.
- Support/access funding must state what money enables.
- The page must be coherent in every status, including empty states.

## Phase 1 Page Shape

The minimum Phase 1 public page should include:

1. Field status, title, Seed, and reason-for-now.
2. Soil summary.
3. Vessels.
4. Gatherings.
5. Tending prompts and contribution entry.
6. Support/access funding.
7. Emerging Yield.
8. Steward notes.
9. Commons Return plan.

## Hard Boundaries

- Do not lead with books for sale.
- Do not present Gatherings as detached events.
- Do not use comments or social posting as the main interaction.
- Do not publish Contributions without review.
- Do not hide consent copy behind legal language.
- Do not let support CTAs imply editorial influence.
