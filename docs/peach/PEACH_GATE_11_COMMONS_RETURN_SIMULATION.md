# PEACH Gate 11 Commons Return Simulation

Date: 2026-07-31

This is a manual simulation only. No Yield was publicly released and no Commons entry was published.

## Field 001 summary

Field 001 asks: What does a community learn when it studies itself as seriously as institutions study it?

The rehearsal contributions tested whether participants can respond with adult-safe, non-sensitive reflections and whether stewards can review those records without making them public.

## Rehearsal contribution-to-Yield simulation

- Contribution 1, `held`: could become a private method note after follow-up, but should not be quoted or summarized externally yet.
- Contribution 2, `accepted_private`: could inform an internal synthesis about community self-questioning, subject to consent scope and follow-up before any public use.
- Contribution 3, `rejected`: remains private and excluded from any Yield simulation.

## Possible Phase 1 Yield

A Phase 1 Yield could be a short internal Field report with:

- the Field seed and reason for now;
- a method note on self-study;
- a summary of recurring safe themes;
- a source trail for Vessels used;
- consent scope summary;
- questions for a second Field;
- no raw private contribution bodies.

## Contributions that remain private

All rehearsal contributions remain private. `held` and `rejected` records must not be used. `accepted_private` does not mean public approval.

## Consent checks required before inclusion

Before any real Yield or Commons Return:

- confirm contributor identity/contact;
- confirm consent level allows the intended use;
- confirm credit preference;
- confirm visibility preference;
- confirm Yield permission;
- perform follow-up for `follow_up_required` or `follow_up_before_crediting`;
- verify no youth/sensitive/third-party private material is present;
- record steward decision and audit evidence.

## Commons Return path

A future Commons Return would publish only approved, consented synthesis and source trail material. It would include attribution and consent scope notes and would link to a public Commons entry only after steward release.
